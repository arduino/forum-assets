Discussion about the **Arduino USB Camera**

The **Arduino USB Camera** is a plug-and-play high resolution video camera suitable for adding vision capabilities to Arduino's hybrid boards.

Built around the Sony IMX376 image sensor, it captures up to 3840 × 2160 (UHD 4K) video and is recognized automatically as a standard USB Video Class (UVC) device, with no drivers to install. It features an autofocus lens, an integrated LED ring light, and a built-in microphone.
