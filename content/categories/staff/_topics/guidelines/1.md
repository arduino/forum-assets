Arduino Forum is a community dedicated to the mission of making [embedded systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embedded_system) accessible to everyone.

This document provides information about the appropriate way to participate in this amazing community. Please take the time to review it.

For information about using the forum, please also take a look at our ["**How to get the best out of this forum**" guide](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/how-to-get-the-best-out-of-this-forum/679966).

## [Arduino Forum Rules](#arduino-forum-rules)

We hold our members to certain standards of behavior. These rules allow Arduino Forum to be effective as a place for learning and sharing knowledge.

The rules are enforced by our team of friendly moderators. You can recognize moderators by the presence of a shield icon next to the username at the top of their posts and forum profile:

### [Be Polite](#be-polite)

Nothing sabotages a healthy conversation like rudeness:

- Be civil. Don't post anything that a reasonable person would consider offensive, abusive, or hate speech.
- Keep it clean. Don't post anything profane, obscene, or sexually explicit.

If you're unsure, ask yourself how you would feel if your post was featured on the front page of a major news site.

Our goal is for the forum content to serve as a resource for people of all ages and ways of life. Keep the language, links, and images you use here clean and safe.

You may wish to respond by disagreeing. That's fine. But remember to _criticize ideas, not people_. Please avoid:

- Name-calling
- Ad hominem attacks
- Responding to a post's tone instead of its actual content

If you encounter impolite behavior, _do not respond in kind_. Instead, [raise a flag](#flag-problems), then leave the matter to the moderators.

<a name="flag-problems"></a>

### [If You See a Problem, Flag It](#flag-problems)

When you see bad behavior, don't reply. Replying encourages bad behavior by acknowledging it, consumes your energy, and wastes everyone's time. [_Just flag it_](https://meta.discourse.org/t/flag-a-post-for-moderator-attention/32783).

Arduino Forum's team of human moderators promptly reviews every flag that is raised.

A flag may result in moderation or disciplinary actions visible to you, or it may result in the initiation of internal processes. Rest assured that the moderation team cares deeply about keeping Arduino Forum safe and clean, and will give your report proper attention.

<a name="illegal-content"></a>

#### [Illegal Content](#illegal-content)

If you see illegal content on **Arduino Forum**, [flag](https://meta.discourse.org/t/flag-a-post-for-moderator-attention/32783) the post and select "**It's Illegal**" from the list of reasons for flagging in the dialog that appears.

If you don't have an **Arduino Forum** account, you can report illegal content here:

https://report-content.arduino.cc/

### [Correctly Categorize Your Topic](#correctly-categorize-your-topic)

In order to make it easier for people to find discussions on subjects of interest, we organize [forum topics](https://meta.discourse.org/t/understanding-discourse-for-new-users/96331#p-468221-basic-terms-used-by-discourse-2) by subject matter. When you create a new topic on Arduino Forum, you must [select a forum category for the topic](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/how-to-get-the-best-out-of-this-forum/679966#1-choose-the-right-category). Please take the time to select the category that best matches the primary subject matter of your topic. You will see a short description of the purpose of each category in the menu. You may find that none of the categories is a perfect match for the specific subject. Just pick the one that is the best fit.

---

⚠ It is never appropriate to select the "**Uncategorized**" category for your topic.

---

<a name="language-specific-categories"></a>

#### [Language-Specific Categories](#language-specific-categories)

Forum categories are provided for forum members who prefer to conduct discussions in certain non-English languages. If you wish to post in one of the languages for which we provide a language-specific category, you must use that category.

---

⚠ If you are using an automatic translation feature in your web browser, pay attention to the language in which the original forum content is written.

---

If you prefer to communicate in a non-English language for which we do not provide a language-specific category, use the primary forum categories. We do recommend posting in English in this case (you are welcome to use an automatic translation service for this purpose), but all languages are allowed.

<a name="dont-crosspost"></a>

### [Don't Crosspost](#dont-crosspost)

Crossposting is the practice of posting about the same thing in multiple places. Crossposting is not allowed on Arduino Forum. The reason for this is that it tends to waste the time of the forum helpers. A helper might spend a significant amount of time responding to your crosspost without realizing that someone has already made an equivalent effort in another forum [topic](https://meta.discourse.org/t/understanding-discourse-for-new-users/96331#p-468221-basic-terms-used-by-discourse-2).

Pick the most appropriate place for your post, and then make the single post there. The forum helpers closely monitor the forum activity, so will be sure to see your post.

We would generally prefer users don't crosspost across multiple sites, for the same reason that crossposting within Arduino Forum is not allowed. If you insist on inter-site crossposting, please include a link to the post on the other site in your forum post so that the helpers can check the activity there.

<a name="dont-hijack"></a>

### [Don't Hijack](#dont-hijack)

Hijacking is the practice of co-opting someone else's forum [topic](https://meta.discourse.org/t/understanding-discourse-for-new-users/96331#p-468221-basic-terms-used-by-discourse-2) for your own divergent purposes. Hijacking is not allowed on Arduino Forum. The reason is that this may interfere with the support both the original topic creator and the hijacker hope to receive.

Everyone is welcome to participate in existing forum discussions with on-topic replies, but these replies must serve to further the topic creator's goals. If you want assistance with something, or want to initiate a discussion that won't further those goals, just create a new topic. If the existing topic is relevant to your own, you are welcome to link to the existing topic.

<a name="stay-on-topic"></a>

### [Stay On Topic](#stay-on-topic)

Replies to a topic should serve to further the topic creator's goals. Overly tangential or completely off-topic replies can have a harmful effect by confusing the person seeking assistance, or wasting the time of other participants who wish to focus on the primary subject matter.

It is easy to wander off onto tangential discussion of interesting subjects. You are always welcome to create a dedicated forum topic for such discussions. If the original forum topic is relevant to that discussion, you can link to it in your post.

If you see off-topic posts, refrain from replying to them. [Flag the inciting post](#flag-problems) and continue the on-topic discussion. If the tangent is something you find of interest, you can request that the moderators split the off-topic posts into a dedicated forum topic, in which further discussion on that tangent will be free to proceed.

<a name="conserve-the-conversation"></a>

### [Conserve the Conversation](#conserve-the-conversation)

The posts in a forum topic provide the context for the replies that follow. It is fine to make trivial edits to your posts, but significant edits or deletion would diminish the efforts of the other participants in the conversation by causing a loss of meaning of their contributions.

When you need to add information, or make a correction to something you wrote in a previous post which has already become part of the discussion, just add a new reply to the topic instead of editing or deleting your previous post.

<a name="anonymization"></a>

#### [Anonymization](#anonymization)

If you decide you no longer wish your username to be associated with posts you made, we will be happy to anonymize them. Just send a request to [privacy@arduino.cc](mailto:privacy@arduino.cc).

<a name="dont-spam"></a>

### [Don't Spam](#dont-spam)

Use of Arduino Forum to advertise products or services is not allowed. If you are only here to advertise, you are in the wrong place and are only wasting your own time (we are very good at detection and removal of spam and spammers).

The exception is that we do permit advertisement of relevant products and services of interest to the Arduino Forum community in the #community:products-and-services category.

<a name="use-ai-responsibly"></a>

### [Use AI Responsibly](#use-ai-responsibly)

Forum members are welcome to request assistance with any content from any source, including AI generated code, etc.

The standard is different for helpers. When providing support here, only share information that is either from an authoritative source (e.g., a manufacturer datasheet), or that you have personally verified. Cursorily copy/pasting content from any non-authoritative source without taking the time to verify the accuracy of that information is irresponsible and not allowed here. This includes content produced by AI. You are welcome to utilize AI as a tool to facilitate support work here, but this is a place of humans helping humans.

If you see what you believe to be irresponsible use of AI, please [flag the post](#flag-problems). As with any other type of rule violation, leave enforcement to the moderators and do not derail the topic with accusations.

<a name="dont-make-multiple-accounts"></a>

### [Don't Make Multiple Accounts](#dont-make-multiple-accounts)

Unnecessary creation of multiple forum accounts by a single person is not allowed. Even when there wasn't malicious intent behind the creation of the accounts, they can still result in confusion.

If you are experiencing a problem with your existing account, send a DM to [the forum administrator](https://forum.arduino.cc/about), or [contact Arduino Support](https://www.arduino.cc/en/contact-us).
